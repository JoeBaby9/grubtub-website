The Ultimate Date Idea on the South Coast

3 min read · Padanaram Harbor, South Dartmouth, MA

Dinner and a movie is fine. A sunset on the water with your own private boat is better. If you are looking for a date idea on the South Coast that actually stands out, here is how to do it on a GrubTub.

The plan

Book the sunset slot, 4:30 to 7:30 PM. You get your own donut boat on Padanaram Harbor for 3 hours, with a table in the middle and an umbrella overhead. You drive it yourself (21+ with a photo ID, no license needed), cruise the harbor north of the Padanaram Bridge, and anchor up as the sun goes down over the water.

It is $300 + taxes & fees for the whole boat. It is the same price for two people or eight, so a double date splits nicely.

Set the table

  • Propane grill, $15: cook steak tips, shrimp skewers or veggie kebabs at the table.
  • Ice-packed cooler, $7: skip the grill and keep a bottle of something cold.
  • Dockside Delivery, $26.50: order from Farm & Coast Market and we bring it to the boat, so you don't even have to cook.

Bring a small speaker, a blanket for when the breeze picks up, and you are set.

Want someone else to drive?

Add the Hosted GrubTub Experience ($150) and a licensed captain handles the boat while you two enjoy the view.

Wednesday option

On summer Wednesdays our Concert Night rental runs 5:30 to 8:30 PM ($350 + taxes & fees) from June 16 to August 25. Anchor up and listen to live music from the water. It makes an easy, low-pressure first or second date.

Tips from the dock

  1. Book early. Weekend sunsets go first.
  2. Arrive at 75 Gulf Road, South Dartmouth, 15 minutes early for check-in.
  3. Plan your photo for the last half hour, when the light is best.
  4. Walk Padanaram village afterward for dessert.

Make it a gift

Anniversary or birthday coming up? A GrubTub gift card never expires, so they can pick the night. Through December 24, gift cards of $250 or more include a free Party Bundle.
