Boat Rental Gift Cards

Padanaram Harbor · 75 Gulf Road, South Dartmouth, MA

Looking for a gift that is not another thing? A GrubTub gift card is a summer day on the water: a private BBQ boat for up to 8 people, 3 hours on Padanaram Harbor in South Dartmouth, MA. They pick the date, bring their crew and we have the boat ready.

Holiday bonus through December 24

Buy any gift card of $250 or more and we add a free Party Bundle to their trip: an extra iced cooler, cups, shot cups and trash service, set up before they arrive.

How much to put on the card

  • $250 covers a morning or afternoon rental before taxes and fees.
  • $300 covers a sunset rental before taxes and fees.
  • $350 covers a Wednesday Concert Night before taxes and fees.

Want to cover the whole thing? Add a little extra for taxes, fees and add-ons like the propane grill ($15).

Good to know

  • Gift cards never expire.
  • The 2027 season runs May 1 to September 30.
  • No boating license needed. The driver just needs to be 21 or older with a photo ID and stay sober.
  • You get the card by email right after checkout, so it works as a last-minute gift too.
