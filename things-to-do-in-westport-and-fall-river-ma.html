Things to Do in Westport and Fall River, MA This Summer

4 min read · By Joey Chedid, GrubTub owner

Westport and Fall River sit right next door to Dartmouth, and a summer weekend down here can easily cover all three. Here are some favorites from someone who grew up on the South Coast, plus how a GrubTub fits in.

Westport

  • Horseneck Beach. The big state beach on the South Coast, with long stretches of sand and surf. Get there early on hot weekends.
  • Westport Point. A small working harbor with fishing boats and a classic New England feel.
  • Farm stands and local wine. Westport is farm country, and stopping for corn, tomatoes or a tasting is half the fun of the drive.

Fall River

  • Battleship Cove. Walk aboard historic Navy ships on the waterfront. A great pick for kids and history fans.
  • Portuguese food. Fall River is known for it. Plan a lunch stop before you head east.
  • The waterfront. Easy walking along the Taunton River with views of the Braga Bridge.

Add a harbor day in Dartmouth

Padanaram Harbor in South Dartmouth is about 20 minutes from Westport and 25 from Fall River. A GrubTub is a private BBQ donut boat for up to 8 people. You drive it yourself, no license needed, and spend 3 hours cruising, anchoring and grilling in a calm, protected harbor full of sailboats.

A good summer Saturday: beach in Westport in the morning, lunch in Fall River or Padanaram, then a sunset GrubTub (4:30 to 7:30 PM) to end the day on the water.

Prices

Morning and afternoon slots are $250, sunset is $300, and Wednesday Concert Night is $350, all plus taxes and fees, for the whole boat. The season runs May 1 to September 30.
