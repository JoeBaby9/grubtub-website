Birthday Party Ideas Near Dartmouth, MA

3 min read · Padanaram Harbor, South Dartmouth, MA

Restaurants are loud, backyards need cleanup, and everyone has done bowling. If you want a birthday people actually remember, put it on the water. A GrubTub is a private donut boat with a table in the middle, docked in Padanaram Harbor in South Dartmouth. Up to 8 people, 3 hours, your own grill, and nobody else on board.

Why a boat birthday works

  • Everyone sits together. The seats wrap around one table, so the whole group is in the conversation.
  • Bring whatever you want. Cake, snacks, food for the grill, a speaker. It is your boat for 3 hours.
  • It photographs well. Red umbrella, blue water, sunset behind you. You will get the group photo.
  • No experience needed. One adult 21 or older drives, and we teach them at the dock.

Aly booked one for her sister's birthday and wrote on Google: "We grilled, listened to music, had lots of laughs, and explored the harbor. Extremely relaxing fun day."

Pick your time

  • Afternoon, 1:00 to 4:00 PM ($250 + taxes & fees): best for a lunch cookout and sunshine.
  • Sunset, 4:30 to 7:30 PM ($300 + taxes & fees): best for a dinner party and the golden hour photos.
  • Morning, 9:30 AM to 12:30 PM ($250 + taxes & fees): calm water and a quieter harbor, good for families with kids.

Prices are for the whole boat, not per person. Bigger party? Book both boats for the same slot.

Add-ons that make it a party

  • Propane grill, $15: cook burgers, kebabs or hot dogs right at the table.
  • Party Bundle, $53: extra iced cooler, cups, shot cups and trash service, all set up before you arrive.
  • Dockside Delivery, $26.50: order from Farm & Coast Market and we bring it out to the boat.
  • Hosted GrubTub Experience, $150: a licensed captain drives so the birthday person (and everyone else) can relax. Max 6 guests.

Birthday checklist

  1. Book your slot and add the grill or cooler.
  2. Send the waiver link to your guests so everyone signs before the day.
  3. Pack the food, the cake (in a box, it can get breezy), plates and a speaker.
  4. Bring fitted life jackets for any infants or small children. We have them for adults and older kids.
  5. Arrive at 75 Gulf Road, South Dartmouth, 15 minutes early.

Giving it as a gift instead?

A GrubTub gift card lets the birthday person pick their own day. Gift cards never expire, and through December 24 any gift card of $250 or more comes with a free Party Bundle.
