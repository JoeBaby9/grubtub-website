Boat Rentals Near New Bedford, MA, No License Required

3 min read · Padanaram Harbor, South Dartmouth, MA

Most people who want a day on the water near New Bedford run into the same problem. They don't own a boat, they don't have a license, and the charter options are either pricey or shared with strangers. A GrubTub fixes all three. It is a round, stable donut boat with a grill table in the middle, and you drive it yourself around Padanaram Harbor in South Dartmouth, about 15 minutes from downtown New Bedford.

Do you need a license to rent a GrubTub?

No. You don't need a boating license or any experience. The person driving has to be 21 or older, show a photo ID at check-in, and stay sober for the trip. Before you leave the dock we walk your driver through steering, speed and docking, and we stay close by if you need us. Guests tell us it is easier than they expected. As Kristie put it in her Google review, "The boat was easier to navigate than I thought."

If nobody in your group wants to drive, add the Hosted GrubTub Experience at checkout and a licensed captain drives the whole trip. That option is limited to 6 guests.

What exactly is a GrubTub?

Picture a floating picnic table for up to 8 people. The boat is round, sits low and steady on the water, and has a shade umbrella overhead. In the middle is a table where you can add a propane grill or an ice-packed cooler. You bring the food and drinks, we set up the boat, and you spend 3 hours cruising, anchoring and eating on the harbor.

The whole boat is yours. We never put strangers on your rental, so it works for families, birthdays, coworkers or a date.

Times and prices for 2027

The season runs May 1 to September 30. Every rental is 3 hours and the price is for the whole boat, not per person.

  • Morning, 9:30 AM to 12:30 PM: $250 + taxes & fees
  • Afternoon, 1:00 to 4:00 PM: $250 + taxes & fees
  • Sunset, 4:30 to 7:30 PM: $300 + taxes & fees
  • Wednesday Concert Night, 5:30 to 8:30 PM: $350 + taxes & fees (June 16 to August 25)

Sunset slots book first, especially on weekends, so plan ahead if you want one.

Where you can go

You can cruise anywhere in the harbor north of the Padanaram Bridge, including the mooring field and the open water by the beach. That gives you plenty of room to explore, anchor up for lunch, and watch the sailboats come and go. Stay clear of rocky areas and swim beaches and follow normal boating rules.

Getting here from New Bedford

We are at 75 Gulf Road in South Dartmouth, right on Padanaram Harbor. From New Bedford it is a short drive west. Arrive 15 minutes before your start time so we can check you in and give your driver the quick lesson. Late arrivals still end at the scheduled time, so build in a few minutes for parking.

Why people pick a GrubTub over a regular boat rental

  • No license, no experience. If you can drive a golf cart, you can drive a GrubTub.
  • Built for eating together. Everyone faces the table, so it feels like a cookout, not a commute.
  • Private. One group per boat, always.
  • Rain or shine. The boats have a canopy. We only cancel for storms or high winds, and if we cancel you get a full refund or credit.
