Good to know

Questions

Who drives the boat?

Anyone 21 or older with a photo ID. We give your driver a quick lesson at the dock, and the driver must stay sober for the whole trip. Rather not drive? Add the Hosted GrubTub Experience and a licensed captain drives (max 6 guests).

Can kids come?

Yes, all ages can ride. We have life jackets on board for adults and older kids. If you are bringing infants or small children, please bring a fitted life jacket for each of them.

What should we bring?

Your food and drinks. Add the propane grill or an ice-packed cooler at checkout and we set it up before you arrive.

Where do we meet?

75 Gulf Road, South Dartmouth, MA 02748. Look for the GrubTubs at the dock and arrive 15 minutes before your start time. Late arrivals still end at the scheduled time.

What if it rains?

We go rain or shine since the boats have a canopy. We only cancel for storms or high winds, and if we cancel you get a full refund or credit.

What is the cancellation policy?

Cancel at least 72 hours before your rental. With Trip Protection you can cancel or reschedule for any reason up to 24 hours before.

Where can we go?

Anywhere in the harbor north of the Padanaram Bridge, including the moorings and the water sports area. Stay clear of rocky areas and beaches and follow standard boating rules.
