Family-Friendly Activities on Buzzards Bay: GrubTub Donut Boats

3 min read · Padanaram Harbor, South Dartmouth, MA

Finding something the whole family enjoys, from grandparents to toddlers, is hard. A GrubTub makes it easy. It is a private, slow, steady donut boat on Padanaram Harbor in South Dartmouth, with a table in the middle and room for up to 8 people.

Why families like it

  • Calm and steady. The round shape sits low on the water, and the harbor north of the Padanaram Bridge is protected.
  • Shade overhead. Every boat has an umbrella canopy, which matters with little kids on a hot day.
  • Everyone together. The seats wrap around the table, so nobody is stuck at the back of the boat.
  • Lunch on board. Add a propane grill and cook hot dogs and burgers on the water.

Kids and life jackets

All ages can ride. We have life jackets on board for adults and older kids. If you are bringing infants or small children, please bring a properly fitted life jacket for each of them, since child sizing matters and we want them to fit right.

Who drives

One adult drives, and they need to be 21 or older with a photo ID and stay sober for the trip. You don't need a license or experience. Before you leave the dock we show your driver how to steer and dock, and older kids love watching how it works.

Best time for families

The morning slot, 9:30 AM to 12:30 PM, is usually the best fit. The water is calmer, the harbor is quieter, and you are back before nap time. It is $250 + taxes & fees for the whole boat. The afternoon slot (1:00 to 4:00 PM) is the same price if your family runs late.

What to pack

  1. Food and drinks (or add Dockside Delivery from Farm & Coast Market for $26.50).
  2. Sunscreen and hats.
  3. Fitted life jackets for infants and small children.
  4. A towel and a change of clothes for the kids.

Then arrive at 75 Gulf Road, South Dartmouth, 15 minutes before your start time.

Weather

We run rain or shine thanks to the canopy, and only cancel for storms or high winds. If we cancel, you get a full refund or credit. For extra flexibility, add Trip Protection (+15%) to cancel or reschedule for any reason up to 24 hours ahead.
