Party Boat Rental on the South Coast

Padanaram Harbor · 75 Gulf Road, South Dartmouth, MA

A GrubTub is a private party boat built around a table. Up to 8 of you sit facing each other, the grill is in the middle, the music is yours, and you spend 3 hours cruising Padanaram Harbor in South Dartmouth. It is one of the easiest group activities on the South Coast of Massachusetts to plan, and nobody needs boating experience.

Who books GrubTub for a party

  • Birthdays. Bring the cake and the candles. The table makes it feel like a party, not a ride.
  • Bachelorettes and bachelor parties. Matching outfits, a playlist and the whole boat to yourselves.
  • Family reunions and visiting relatives. All ages can ride.
  • Team outings. Weekday slots work well for work groups.

How many people fit?

Each boat holds up to 8 people. Have a bigger group? Ask about booking both boats for the same time slot so your whole crew can cruise side by side. Call or text (774) 264-4904 to set it up.

Make it a party: add-ons at checkout

  • Propane grill, $15. Ready to cook when you step aboard.
  • Party Bundle, $53. Extra iced cooler, cups, shot cups and trash service, set up before you arrive.
  • Dockside Delivery, $26.50. Order from Farm & Coast Market and we bring the food out to your boat.
  • Hosted GrubTub Experience, $150. A licensed captain drives so nobody in the group has to (up to 6 guests).

Prices

One flat price for the whole boat for 3 hours. Split eight ways, a $250 slot is about $31 a person before taxes and fees.

  • Morning, 9:30 AM to 12:30 PM: $250 + taxes & fees
  • Afternoon, 1:00 to 4:00 PM: $250 + taxes & fees
  • Sunset, 4:30 to 7:30 PM: $300 + taxes & fees
  • Wednesday Concert Night, 5:30 to 8:30 PM: $350 + taxes & fees (June 16 to August 25)

The rules that keep it fun

The driver must be 21 or older with a photo ID and stay sober for the whole trip. Everyone else is there to enjoy it. Stay in the harbor north of the Padanaram Bridge and clear of swim areas and rocks. We go rain or shine because the boats have a canopy, and we only cancel for storms or high winds.

Close to New Bedford, Fall River and Providence

GrubTub is at 75 Gulf Road in South Dartmouth, about 15 minutes from New Bedford, 25 from Fall River and 45 from Providence. Easy for a group to meet up, and the village of Padanaram has spots to grab dinner after.

Quick answers

How many people can go on a GrubTub party boat?

Up to 8 people per boat. For bigger groups, ask about booking both boats for the same slot.

Do we need a captain for a party boat rental?

No. Anyone 21 or older with a photo ID can drive after a quick lesson at the dock, as long as they stay sober. You can also add a licensed captain for $150 (up to 6 guests).

Can we bring our own food and drinks?

Yes. You bring the food and drinks. Add a grill, cooler or Party Bundle at checkout and we set it up before you arrive.

How much is a party boat rental near New Bedford?

GrubTub starts at $250 for 3 hours for the whole boat, plus taxes and fees. Sunset is $300 and Wednesday Concert Night is $350.
