Team Building Ideas Near New Bedford and Fall River

3 min read · By Joey Chedid, GrubTub owner

Most team outings blur together: a dinner, a bowling alley, a conference room with pizza. If you manage a team in New Bedford, Fall River, Dartmouth or anywhere on the South Coast, a GrubTub is a different kind of afternoon that people actually talk about after.

Why a GrubTub works for a team

  • Everyone faces each other. The boat is round with a table in the middle, so conversation happens on its own.
  • It's private. Your group gets the whole boat, never shared with strangers.
  • No skills needed. One person drives after a quick lesson at the dock. Prefer that nobody from work drives? Add a licensed captain for $150 (up to 6 guests).
  • Food is built in. Grill lunch on the water, or add Dockside Delivery and have food brought to the boat.

Group size

Each boat holds up to 8 people. For a bigger team, ask about booking both boats for the same time slot so the whole group can cruise together. Call or text (774) 264-4904 to plan it.

Best times for work groups

Weekday mornings (9:30 AM to 12:30 PM) and afternoons (1:00 to 4:00 PM) are the easiest slots to get and fit a work day. A sunset slot (4:30 to 7:30 PM) makes a great after-work outing.

Cost

$250 for a morning or afternoon and $300 for sunset, plus taxes and fees, for the whole boat. With 8 people that's about $31 to $38 a person before taxes and fees, less than most team dinners.

Planning checklist

  • Pick your date and slot and book online.
  • Add a grill, cooler or Party Bundle at checkout.
  • Have every guest sign the waiver from the confirmation email.
  • Choose your driver (21+, photo ID, stays sober) or add a captain.
  • Meet at 75 Gulf Road, South Dartmouth, 15 minutes early.
