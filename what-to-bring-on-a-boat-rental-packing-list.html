What to Bring on a Boat Rental: The GrubTub Packing List

3 min read · By Joey Chedid, GrubTub owner

The best GrubTub trips are the ones where nobody has to run back to the car. Here is what to pack for a 3-hour rental on Padanaram Harbor, what we already have on the boat, and a few things guests always wish they'd brought.

What we provide

  • The boat, ready to go, with a canopy for shade
  • Life jackets for adults and older kids
  • A quick driving lesson at the dock
  • A propane grill ($15) or ice-packed cooler ($7) if you add one at checkout

Food: keep it simple and grill-friendly

  • Pre-made kebabs, burgers or sausages. Prep at home so you are cooking, not chopping, on the water.
  • Tongs, a spatula and a lighter-free plan. The grill is propane, ready when you step aboard.
  • Buns, condiments and snacks in a bag that can get a little wet.
  • Paper plates, napkins and a trash bag. Or add the Party Bundle ($53) and we handle cups and trash service.

Don't want to cook at all? Add Dockside Delivery ($26.50) and food from Farm & Coast Market comes right out to your boat.

Drinks and ice

Bring water and whatever your group drinks, in cans or plastic, not glass. Add the ice-packed cooler or the Party Bundle's extra iced cooler if you want everything cold for 3 hours. Remember the driver must stay sober for the whole trip.

Sun and comfort

  • Sunscreen and sunglasses. The canopy helps, but the water reflects a lot of sun.
  • A light layer for sunset slots. It cools off fast on the harbor in the evening.
  • Towels if anyone plans to swim at anchor.
  • A waterproof phone pouch and a portable speaker.

For kids

All ages can ride. If you're bringing infants or small children, bring a properly fitted life jacket for each one. Snacks and a change of clothes help too.

What to leave at home

  • Glass bottles
  • Anything you'd be upset to drop in the harbor
  • More people than fit. Each boat holds up to 8.

Before you leave the house

Make sure every passenger has signed the waiver from the link in your confirmation email, and that your driver has a photo ID and is 21 or older. Arrive 15 minutes early at 75 Gulf Road, South Dartmouth.

Quick answers

Can we bring our own food and drinks on a GrubTub?

Yes. You bring the food and drinks. We can set up a propane grill or ice-packed cooler before you arrive.

Do I need to bring life jackets?

We have life jackets for adults and older kids. Bring a fitted life jacket for each infant or small child.

Is glass allowed on the boat?

Please bring cans or plastic instead of glass.
