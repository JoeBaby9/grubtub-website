Groups and events

Bring the Whole Crew: Group Outings on Padanaram Harbor

Teams, bachelorettes, birthdays, reunions and wedding weekends. Up to 16 people across 2 private boats.

Plan a group outingCall or text (774) 264-4904

Why groups book GrubTub

  • Private. Your group gets the whole boat for 3 hours, never shared with strangers.
  • Everyone faces each other. The boat is round with a table in the middle, so nobody is stuck at the end of a long table.
  • Food on board. Add a propane grill ($15) or an ice cooler ($7), or have food brought to the boat with Dockside Delivery from Farm & Coast Market.
  • No license needed. Your driver must be 21+ with a photo ID and stay sober. We teach them at the dock.
  • Want everyone to relax? Add a licensed captain for $150 (up to 6 guests per boat).

Group size and pricing

Each boat holds up to 8 people. Book both boats for the same slot and up to 16 people cruise together.

  • Morning or afternoon: $250 per boat, plus taxes and fees. About $31 a person with 8 aboard.
  • Sunset: $300 per boat, plus taxes and fees.
  • Wednesday Concert Night (mid-June to late August): $350 per boat, plus taxes and fees.

Popular group outings

  • Team outings and client events. Weekday slots fit a work day, and sunset works as an after-work event.
  • Bachelorettes and bachelor parties. See our bachelorette guide.
  • Birthdays and graduations. Birthday ideas here.
  • Wedding weekends. A welcome-day activity for guests coming into town.
  • Family reunions. All ages can ride. Bring your own life jackets for infants and small children.

How to book a group

For one boat, book online. For both boats in the same slot, a captain, or anything custom, email us or call or text (774) 264-4904 and we'll set it up. The 2027 season runs May 1 to September 30, and weekends fill first.

Quick answers

How many people can you take?

Up to 8 per boat, so up to 16 when you book both boats for the same slot. Every booking is private, never shared with strangers.

Can a captain drive our group?

Yes. Add the Hosted GrubTub Experience for $150 and a licensed captain drives, up to 6 guests per boat.

Do you do weekday events?

Yes. Weekday mornings (9:30 AM to 12:30 PM) and afternoons (1:00 to 4:00 PM) are the easiest slots to get for work groups.

Is there a boating license requirement?

No. Your driver must be 21+ with a photo ID and stay sober, and we teach them at the dock.
