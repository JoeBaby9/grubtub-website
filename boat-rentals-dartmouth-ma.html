Boat Rentals in Dartmouth, MA

Padanaram Harbor · 75 Gulf Road, South Dartmouth, MA

Looking for a boat rental in Dartmouth, Massachusetts? GrubTub rents private BBQ donut boats right on Padanaram Harbor in South Dartmouth. You drive the boat yourself, bring your own food and drinks, and get 3 hours on one of the prettiest harbors on the South Coast. No boating license or experience needed.

What you get with a GrubTub rental

  • A private boat for up to 8 people. One group per boat, never shared with strangers.
  • A grill table in the middle. Add a propane grill or an ice-packed cooler at checkout and we set it up before you arrive.
  • Shade overhead. Every boat has a canopy, so you are covered from sun and light rain.
  • Life jackets and a driving lesson. We walk your driver through steering and docking before you leave.
  • 3 full hours to cruise, anchor, eat and swim around the harbor.

Prices for the 2027 season

The season runs May 1 to September 30. The price is for the whole boat, not per person, so a group of 8 on a $250 slot pays about $31 each before taxes and fees.

  • Morning, 9:30 AM to 12:30 PM: $250 + taxes & fees
  • Afternoon, 1:00 to 4:00 PM: $250 + taxes & fees
  • Sunset, 4:30 to 7:30 PM: $300 + taxes & fees
  • Wednesday Concert Night, 5:30 to 8:30 PM: $350 + taxes & fees (June 16 to August 25)

Do you need a boating license?

No. The driver needs to be 21 or older with a photo ID and has to stay sober for the trip. We teach them at the dock. GrubTubs are round, stable and slow, built for calm harbor cruising rather than open ocean, which is why first-timers handle them easily. If nobody wants to drive, add the Hosted GrubTub Experience and a licensed captain drives (up to 6 guests).

How GrubTub compares to other boat rentals around Dartmouth

Most boat rentals on the South Coast fall into two groups: powerboats you rent by the hour, which often expect some boating experience, and captained charters, which cost more and run on someone else's plan. GrubTub sits in between. You get your own boat and your own schedule, the price is one flat rate for 3 hours, and the whole thing is built around eating together on the water. It is less about getting somewhere fast and more about a slow, social afternoon on the harbor.

Where you'll cruise

You can go anywhere in Padanaram Harbor north of the Padanaram Bridge, including the mooring field and the open water near the beach. It is protected water with sailboats all around and views back to the village. Most groups cruise for a bit, drop anchor, fire up the grill and take their time.

When to book

Summer weekends and sunset slots go first. If you have a set date, like a birthday or a visit from out-of-town family, book it early. Weekday mornings and afternoons are the easiest to get and are great for families and work outings.
