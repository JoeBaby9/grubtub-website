Bachelorette Party Ideas Near New Bedford, MA: Rent a Donut Boat

3 min read · Padanaram Harbor, South Dartmouth, MA

If you are planning a bachelorette on the South Coast, you want something that feels like an event, keeps the group together, and gives the bride a few great photos. A private GrubTub on Padanaram Harbor checks all three, and it is only 15 minutes from New Bedford.

What you get

A GrubTub is a round donut boat with a table in the center and a red umbrella overhead. It fits up to 8 people, the rental is 3 hours, and the boat is yours alone. You bring the food, drinks and playlist. We set up the boat before you arrive.

Let a captain drive

Normally one person in your group drives, and that person has to be 21+ with a photo ID and stay sober for the whole trip. For a bachelorette, most groups would rather nobody be stuck with that job. Add the Hosted GrubTub Experience ($150) at checkout and a licensed captain drives the entire trip. With a captain on board the limit is 6 guests, which fits most bridal parties.

Best time slot for a bachelorette

Sunset, 4:30 to 7:30 PM, $300 + taxes & fees. You get warm light for photos, an evening on the water, and you are back in time for dinner and a night out. It is also our most requested slot, so book as soon as you have a date. If your group wants a daytime party instead, the 1:00 PM afternoon slot is $250 + taxes & fees.

Make it a party

  • Party Bundle, $53: extra iced cooler, cups, shot cups and trash service, all ready on board.
  • Ice-packed cooler, $7 or propane grill, $15: pick one for the center of the table.
  • Dockside Delivery, $26.50: food from Farm & Coast Market brought out to the boat.

Match your outfits, bring leis or sashes, and plan the group photo for when the sun is low behind you.

Planning checklist

  1. Pick your date and book the sunset slot early.
  2. Add the captain and the Party Bundle at checkout.
  3. Share the waiver link so every guest signs ahead of time.
  4. Consider Trip Protection (+15%) so you can cancel or reschedule for any reason up to 24 hours before.
  5. Arrive at 75 Gulf Road, South Dartmouth, 15 minutes before your start time.

What if it rains?

We go rain or shine because the boats have a canopy. We only cancel for storms or high winds, and if we cancel you get a full refund or credit.
