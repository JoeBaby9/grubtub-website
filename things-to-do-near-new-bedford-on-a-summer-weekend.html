Things to Do Near New Bedford on a Summer Weekend

3 min read · Padanaram Harbor, South Dartmouth, MA

New Bedford and the towns around it are at their best in summer. If you have a weekend to fill, here is a mix of ideas, from classic sightseeing to something your group probably hasn't done before.

Explore the city's waterfront history

New Bedford's whaling history is everywhere downtown. The New Bedford Whaling National Historical Park covers the cobblestone streets and historic buildings near the harbor, and it is an easy place to spend a morning on foot. Fort Taber Park in the South End is good for a walk along the water with views out over Buzzards Bay.

Spend a beach day

The South Coast has some of the best beaches in Massachusetts. Horseneck Beach in Westport is the big one, with wide sand and open ocean. Go early on hot weekends because it fills up fast.

Get your group on the water

If you are planning for a group, this is where we come in. A GrubTub is a private donut boat with a grill table in the middle, docked in Padanaram Harbor in South Dartmouth, about 15 minutes from New Bedford. You drive it yourself (no license needed, the driver just has to be 21+ with ID), bring your own food and drinks, and spend 3 hours cruising, anchoring and grilling.

It works especially well for groups because everyone sits around one table facing each other. It feels more like a backyard cookout than a boat ride. Up to 8 people fit on a boat, and the price is for the whole boat:

  • Morning or afternoon: $250 + taxes & fees
  • Sunset: $300 + taxes & fees
  • Wednesday Concert Night: $350 + taxes & fees

Bigger group? We have two boats, so you can book both for the same time slot and cruise together.

What a group GrubTub day looks like

  1. Book online and pick a propane grill ($15) or an ice-packed cooler ($7).
  2. Everyone signs the waiver on their phone before the trip.
  3. Arrive at 75 Gulf Road 15 minutes early for check-in and a quick driving lesson.
  4. Head out for 3 hours: cruise the harbor, anchor up, grill, play music.

Add the Party Bundle ($53) and we set up an extra iced cooler, cups, shot cups and trash service before you arrive, so all you carry on is the food.

Finish with sunset

If your weekend has room for one evening plan, make it the sunset. The 4:30 PM GrubTub slot has you on the water as the light goes down over the harbor. It is the most popular time we run, so book it early.
